How it works

Meet at real sessions, not in an endless feed.

Join your club

Sign up for sessions run by your city’s club: long runs, climb nights, tennis socials, rides, swims, yoga, and hikes.

Train together

Show up and check in with the organizer’s code. Afterwards, see the people who were actually there.

Then match

Like a photo or a whole profile. If it’s mutual, a chat opens, with date ideas built from what you share.
