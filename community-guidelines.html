Community Guidelines

Version 1.0
The short version
  • Be real: your name, your age, your photos.
  • Be respectful: no harassment and no sexual content.
  • Never ask anyone for money.
  • Report anything wrong. We act within 24 hours.
01

Be real

Use your real first name, your real age, and recent photos of yourself. No fake profiles, no photos of other people, no celebrities.

02

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or unwanted sexual messages. No nudity or sexual content anywhere on Rally. No pressure: a like is not a promise.

03

Keep it safe

Never ask for money, gift cards, or financial details. No scams, spam, or selling. Don’t share anyone else’s private information.

04

Show up well

At club sessions, follow the organizer and the venue’s rules. Training together is the point; don’t use a session to pressure anyone.

05

Adults only

Rally is for people 18 and over. Report anyone you believe is under 18.

06

If something’s wrong

Report it from the profile or chat menu, and block the person. We review every report within 24 hours. In an emergency, contact local emergency services first.

07

Consequences

Breaking these guidelines gets content removed and accounts banned. Serious cases may be reported to the authorities.

Questions about Rally or your data: support@rallyapp.ca
